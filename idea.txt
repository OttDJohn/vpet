A virtual pet, right now just for feature phones. We need a server backend
because there's no way to consistently run a background process on there and
send push notifications when something is up.

So in your phone you've got a pet. The pet is from some species or another.
It's got a couple of stats. Still nailing them down but definitely food and
either training or fun. Maybe also some sort of stamina thing.

For the most part you just feed and train your pet, but the 'gameplay' is
exploration. You send your pet to a destination with a short, medium, or long
duration. Short durations are for gathering resources. Long durations get
decorations and other rewards. Medium is a mix.

If your pet comes back with decorations, they can be used to decorate the
habitat.

If your pet ever hits -5 food or fun, it dies. This includes while on a trip.
It can also die of old age.

If your pet dies you can hatch a new egg from the ones you have available.
